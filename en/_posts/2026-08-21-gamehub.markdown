description: |
  [GameHub — GayStation 3 Party Pack](https://github.com/barnabas47/gamehub) is a 2-player local party game collection featuring 7 full-featured arcade games built in C# WPF with an authentic PS3 CrossMediaBar (XMB) menu interface, animated silk wave shader, and floating particle effects.

  **Main features:**
  - **PS3 CrossMediaBar (XMB) UI**: Authentic menu system with animated silk wave, floating particles, and "GayStation 3" startup screen.
  - **7 Unique 2-Player Party Games**:
    1. **AZ Tanks**: Bouncing bullet maze tank combat.
    2. **Stick Fight**: 2D platformer stickman brawl.
    3. **Penguin Sumo**: Slippery ice arena bump match with special abilities.
    4. **Air Hockey**: High-speed neon air hockey physics.
    5. **Duck Game**: Arcade duck duel with weapons and quacking.
    6. **Micro Kart 2D**: Split-screen Mario Kart style kart racing with 5 thematic tracks and power-up items (Banana, Shells, Mushroom, Lightning, Star).
    7. **Nidhogg 2D**: Fencing duel with 3 stance heights, parries, sword throw, dodge roll, and giant Nidhogg dragon execution.
  - **Customizable Player Profiles**: Custom names and colors.
  - **Custom Sound Effects**: Synthesized audio feedback and arcade SFX.

  **Technologies:**
  - Language & Framework: C# (.NET 8.0), WPF (Windows Presentation Foundation)
  - UI & Graphics: XAML, Custom Canvas Rendering, DispatcherTimer game loop
  - Audio: System.Windows.Media, procedural sound effects

  **How to run:**
  - Open the project in Visual Studio or navigate to the directory and run: `dotnet run`
